Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 1/31/25

1. Introduction

Welcome to Cinders Pizza! Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website https://www.cinderspizza.com/. By using our website, you agree to the terms outlined in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information when you visit our website:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, and any other details you provide when placing an order, signing up for newsletters, or contacting us.
  • Payment Information: If you make a purchase, your payment details may be collected by our third-party payment processor.
  • Automatically Collected Information: IP address, browser type, pages visited, and other usage data through cookies and similar tracking technologies.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process and fulfill your orders.
  • Respond to inquiries and customer service requests.
  • Improve our website and services.
  • Send promotional offers, updates, and newsletters (if you opt-in).
  • Detect, prevent, and address fraud or security issues.

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. However, we may share your information with:

  • Service Providers: Third-party vendors that help us operate our website, process payments, or provide other necessary services.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law, regulation, or legal process, we may disclose your information.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of our business, your information may be transferred.

5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your browsing experience. You can adjust your browser settings to refuse cookies, but some features of our website may not function properly.

6. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, including our ice cream shop, Taste & See Creamery. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites. Please review their privacy policies separately.

7. Data Security

We implement security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or alteration. However, no online transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Rights and Choices

You may have rights under applicable privacy laws, such as:

  • Requesting access to your personal data.
  • Requesting corrections or deletions of your data.
  • Opting out of marketing communications.

To exercise these rights, please contact us at contact@cinderspizza.com.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
