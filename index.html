Pizza

(10 inch personal size)

All of our pizzas start with our signature house-made cold-proof dough, crafted for the perfect balance of flavor and texture. We pair it with house-made sauces, hand-sliced fresh mozzarella, and flavorful, locally sourced produce from the California and Oregon region. Our menu brings a mix of reimagined classics and bold new creations—including some featuring our own signature Hot Honey. For the pizza-obsessed (like us), our crust is best described as "neo-Neapolitan"—soft on the bottom, perfectly fire-toasted around the edges, and irresistibly delicious.
